# Easier levels for the Torres del Paine days (days 13–15)

Research for adding **Easy / Moderate / Full** to the three Hotel Las Torres hike days. Research date: 28 Sep 2026.
"Full" is the plan that's already in the guide. Figures come from the sources named. **[unverified]** marks anything I could not confirm from a primary source.

Level targets used (from the brief):
- **Easy:** about 8 km or less, 250 m or less, nothing steep or exposed. Vehicle tours, boats, horses and rest days count.
- **Moderate:** about half of Full (8–13 km, 500 m or less), or a turnaround point on the Full route.

## How Las Torres runs excursions (this limits how the group can split)

- The programme includes excursions inside the park with bilingual guides: "11 full and 10 half-day tours". Boat trips ("navigations") are not included. Source: [lastorres.com all-inclusive vacation page](https://lastorres.com/en/all-inclusive/all-inclusive-patagonia-vacation/).
- Booking: in advance through the reservation executive or the excursions department, or at the hotel **until 20:00 the day before**. Same source.
- **Minimum group size: 6 (can drop to 4 in low season)** for an excursion to run. Source: [Going Awesome Places review](https://goingawesomeplaces.com/hotel-las-torres-review/). This is a second-hand source, not Las Torres.
- What this means for 19 people: the group can split into **three levels of at least 6 people each** (for example 6 + 6 + 7) on the same day. A level with fewer than 6 people would have to join a group from other hotel guests on the same excursion, or ask Las Torres for an exception.
- No source says how many guests one guide takes, or whether one excursion can have a "turnaround" subgroup with its own guide. **Ask Las Torres** (see the questions at the end).
- Every Las Torres excursion page says "All our excursions are exclusive to guests of Hotel Las Torres". The CLP prices on the pages below are the stand-alone rates. For guests on the programme, the excursions are included. Only the Grey boat costs extra.

Main excursion list sources:
- [tools.lastorres.com/excursions-videos](https://tools.lastorres.com/excursions-videos) (Las Torres's own list: 16 items with km, hours and difficulty)
- [Swoop Patagonia: Excursions from Hotel Las Torres](https://www.swoop-patagonia.com/chile/torres-del-paine/hotels/torres/excursions) (14 items. The figures mostly match the Las Torres pages)
- No PDF excursion list was found on lastorres.com.

## Day 13 — Tue 1 Dec · Base Torres

**Full (current plan):** Base Torres from the hotel. The guide says 19.1 km and 975 m. Las Torres says **18.8 km, 8 h, difficulty High, viewpoint at 890 m** ([Las Torres Base page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/las-torres-base/), [tools list](https://tools.lastorres.com/excursions-videos)). Other sources: 19.7 km and 1,217 m ([10Adventures](https://www.10adventures.com/hikes/torres-del-paine-national-park/mirador-las-torres-from-the-torres-del-paine-welcome-center/)), 18 km and 900 m ([The Outbound](https://www.theoutbound.com/chile/hiking/hike-mirador-las-torres): 11.2 mi, 3,000 ft). The guide's own GPX (`gpx/day-13.gpx`) measures 19.1 km and about 1,000 m of total climb. Keep Full as it is.

### Recommended Easy — Azul Lagoon half day (Las Torres)
- **What you do:** Van to Laguna Azul on the north-east side of the park. A short walk with a full view of the three Towers, plus a stop at the Paine waterfall. Guanacos are common.
- **Distance:** 1 km walking ("30-minute easy hike"). **Climbing:** not stated. The walk is flat per the description **[unverified]**. **Time:** 4 h total, 1.5 h of it in the van. **Difficulty:** Easy.
- **On the Las Torres list:** Yes. Stand-alone rate CLP 153,000.
- **Why:** It is the one easy option where you still see the Towers on the day everyone else hikes to them.
- **Sources:** [lastorres.com Azul Lagoon](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/azul-lagoon/), [tools list](https://tools.lastorres.com/excursions-videos).
- **Alternatives at the same level (all Las Torres, all half day):**
  - Ascencio Valley horse ride: 7 km, 3 h, Easy, "suitable for all skill levels".
  - Lake Nordenskjöld horse ride: 8 km, 3 h, Easy.
  - Baqueano cultural experience: 1.5–3 h (sources differ), Low.
  - Biointensive garden: 1 h.

  Any two of these fill a relaxed day at the hotel. Sources: [tools list](https://tools.lastorres.com/excursions-videos), [Swoop](https://www.swoop-patagonia.com/chile/torres-del-paine/hotels/torres/excursions), [Baqueano page](https://sustentabilidad.lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/baqueano-experience/).

### Recommended Moderate — Base Torres trail, turning back at Chileno
- **What you do:** The same trail as Full, from the hotel up the Ascencio Valley over Windy Pass to the Chileno sector, then back the same way. Chileno is a camp run by Las Torres ([lastorres.com Chileno camp](https://lastorres.com/en/camping/camping-chileno/)). The steepest climb of the first half (to Windy Pass) is included. The forest section and the final boulder slope ("the final, yet hardest, section" per Las Torres) are skipped.
- **Distance:** **8.8–10.7 km** round trip, depending on source:
  - Las Torres horse+hike excursion: riding to Chileno is 9.2 km round trip ([page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/las-torres-viewpoint-horseback-riding-hiking/)).
  - PeakVisor: 4.4 km each way ([Refugio Chileno](https://peakvisor.com/poi/refugio-chileno.html)).
  - The guide's GPX: 5.3 km each way from the park access point to the nearest track point to Chileno's coordinates.
- **Climbing:** **about 370–420 m on the way up**. PeakVisor: +419 m / −126 m. GPX: +370 m / −94 m. Chileno is at 419 m and Windy Pass at about 455–468 m. Total climb for the round trip is about **460–545 m** (the way back climbs about 90–125 m). This is right at the Moderate ceiling.
- **Time:** about 2 h up ("2 hours from the hotel", [Swoop Hotel Las Torres page](https://www.swoop-patagonia.com/chile/torres-del-paine/hotels/torres); PeakVisor 2 h 15). The full round trip is about **4–5 h with a break [estimate, unverified]**.
- **Difficulty:** Moderate. The pull up to Windy Pass is steady and often very windy. No scrambling.
- **On the Las Torres list:** **No, not as its own excursion.** It only works if Las Torres gives this subgroup its own guide within the Base Torres excursion, or runs it as a separate group of 6 or more. **Ask.**
- **Extra cost:** none expected if Las Torres agrees. Food and drink at Chileno would cost extra **[unverified whether it is open to day hikers in early December]**.
- **Fallback if Las Torres says no:** **Native Lenga Forest** (Las Torres, half day). It follows part of the O Circuit from the hotel into old lenga forest and back along the Paine River. **8 km, 3–4 h, Low–Mid** ([page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/lenga-forest/)). The tools list rates it "Easy, 3 h". Climbing is not published **[unverified]**. This is the guide's existing "native lenga forest walk" alternative, and it is confirmed. On its own it sits between Easy and Moderate. Pair it with a horse ride or the Baqueano experience for a fuller day.

## Day 14 — Wed 2 Dec · Full Paine & Lago Grey

**Check the current figures first.** The guide shows about 9 km and 178 m for this day. Las Torres gives **4 km of walking, 7–10 h, Easy** ([Full Paine – Glacier Grey page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/full-paine-glacier-grey/)). Its tools list even says 1 km. The guide's `gpx/day-14.gpx` is a Wikiloc track titled "Guardería Pudeto – Salto Grande – Mirador …". It measures 9.0 km and about 200 m, going from the Pudeto ranger station out to Mirador Cuernos. That hike is not part of the Full Paine stops listed by Las Torres: Black Bridge, Nordenskjöld lookout, Sarmiento lookout, Lago Pehoé, Salto Grande, and Grey. So the 9 km / 178 m most likely overstates this day. **Recommend correcting Full to "about 4–6 km, under 100 m"** and confirming with Las Torres.

The day is already Easy by Las Torres's rating. The levels differ only in what people do after lunch at Lago Grey.

### Recommended Easy — Full Paine with viewpoints only, or the Grey III boat
- **What you do:** The van tour with the short roadside stops only. After lunch, either stay at the Lago Grey area or take the Grey III boat to the glacier.
- **Stops near the road:**
  - **Salto Grande:** 10–15 min walk from the car park, about 1.4 km round trip ([The Photo Hikes](https://thephotohikes.com/mirador-cuernos-hike/), [AllTrails via search](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/chile/magallanes/mirador-salto-grande)).
  - **Nordenskjöld and Sarmiento lookouts, Lago Pehoé, Black Bridge:** described as van stops. The walking distance at each is not published **[unverified; assumed a few minutes]**.
- **Distance:** about 1–2 km without the boat. With the boat, add the Grey beach walk to the jetty: **30–45 min each way** on flat shingle and sand, about 1.3 km each way ([Hotel Lago Grey navigation](https://www.lagogrey.com/en/navigation/), [Moon via search](https://www.moontravelguides.com/travel/outdoors/parque-nacional-torres-del-paine-hiking-climbing/)). **Climbing:** negligible. **Time:** 7–10 h day, mostly in the van. **Difficulty:** Easy.
- **On the Las Torres list:** Yes (Full Paine – Glacier Grey).
- **Boat cost (extra, not included):**
  - Grey III 2026–27: **CLP 120,000 per adult, round trip**. Departures **09:30, 13:00, 16:00**. About 2 h 45 min sailing. Check-in 1 h before ([lagogrey.com](https://www.lagogrey.com/en/navigation/)).
  - Las Torres lists this tour at CLP 270,000 without the boat and CLP 380,000 with it. That difference is CLP 110,000. The boat "generally departs at 1 PM" and is "an external service" ([Las Torres page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/full-paine-glacier-grey/)).
  - Book early. It is weather-dependent.

### Recommended Moderate — Full Paine with the Lago Grey beach and peninsula walk (= the standard tour)
- **What you do:** The full tour. After lunch, walk across Grey beach to the peninsula viewpoint (Mirador Isla de los Hielos) to see the icebergs and the glacier in the distance.
- **Distance:** peninsula walk **5.3 km round trip, +60 m, 1.5–2 h, Easy** ([The Photo Hikes – Mirador de los Hielos](https://thephotohikes.com/mirador-de-los-hielos-hike)). Las Torres says "approximately 1.5 hours". Day total is about 6–7 km with the roadside stops. **Climbing:** under 100 m. **Time:** 7–10 h. **Difficulty:** Easy.
- **On the Las Torres list:** Yes. This is option (a) of the included tour.

### Full — does it stay as is?
Yes as an itinerary, but it is the same as Moderate. There is no harder version of this tour on the list. Options:
- **Show two levels on this day** (Easy and Moderate/Full together). This is the most honest option.
- Or make Full **Full Paine + Mirador Cuernos**. That adds about 6.4 km and 150 m from the Salto Grande car park ([The Photo Hikes](https://thephotohikes.com/mirador-cuernos-hike/)). It is **not on Las Torres's Full Paine itinerary [unverified whether guides add it]**, and it would take time from the Grey stop.

## Day 15 — Thu 3 Dec · French Valley

**What Full actually is.** The guide's GPX (`gpx/day-15.gpx`) is 18.8 km and about 690 m. Its high point is 486 m, about 9.4 km from Paine Grande at the "French Valley Viewpoint" waypoint. That fits **Mirador Francés, not Mirador Británico**:
- Travel Yes Please gives Paine Grande → Mirador Francés and back as 18.5 km and 549 m ([source](https://www.travelyesplease.com/travel-blog-french-valley-day-hike-torres-del-paine/)).
- Británico is further up the valley, about another 4 km beyond Francés (about 1.5 h) per [a search summary of torresdelpaine.com trail notes](https://torresdelpaine.com/en/tourist-attraction/trail-through-valle-frances/) **[unverified figure]**.
- Las Torres's own tour is **20 km, 12 h (1.5 h van, 1 h boat), High**, with the end point described as a glacier viewpoint ([Las Torres French Valley page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/full-day-tour-of-valle-del-frances/)).
- **Recommend: label Full as "to Mirador Francés"** and confirm with Las Torres. Drop "Británico(?)".

**Catamaran (Pudeto ↔ Paine Grande), from [catamaranpehoe.com](https://catamaranpehoe.com/precios-y-horarios)** (official site. Tickets are CLP 28,000 per leg per person. The Pehoé boat is included in the Las Torres French Valley excursion per the booking):

| Season 2026–27 | Pudeto → Paine Grande | Paine Grande → Pudeto |
|---|---|---|
| English page, "November 2026 – March" | 08:30 · 10:30 · 16:15 · 18:00 | 09:20 · 11:20 · 17:00 · 18:40 |
| Spanish page, "16 Oct 2026 – 31 Mar 2027" | 08:00 · 10:30 · 16:15 · 18:00 | 08:40 · 11:20 · 17:00 · 18:40 |

- **The two language versions disagree on the first boat (08:00/08:40 vs 08:30/09:20).** Confirm with Las Torres or the operator.
- The crossing takes about 30 min ([Travel Yes Please](https://www.travelyesplease.com/travel-blog-french-valley-day-hike-torres-del-paine/)).
- **What this means for a turnaround group:** after the first boat out, the next useful boats back are **17:00 or 18:40** (the 11:20 is too early for any hike). A shorter walk does **not** get people home sooner. They wait at Paine Grande (refuge and café) for the same afternoon boat. A group that doesn't want a 12-hour day is better off on a different excursion than on a shortened French Valley.

### Recommended Easy — Los Cuernos Viewpoint half day (Las Torres)
- **What you do:** Van to Pudeto (the same area as the French Valley boat pier). Walk past Salto Grande waterfall to Mirador Cuernos on the shore of Lago Nordenskjöld, looking straight at Los Cuernos and into the French Valley the others are hiking. Van back to the hotel.
- **Distance:** **6 km** (Las Torres). Other measurements: 6.4 km from the Salto Grande car park, 5 km ([Stingy Nomads](https://stingynomads.com/best-day-hikes-torres-del-paine-chile/)), and 9 km from the Pudeto ranger station (the guide's current day-14 GPX). **Climbing:** about **100–150 m** ([The Photo Hikes](https://thephotohikes.com/mirador-cuernos-hike/): 150 m; another guide cited in search: 100 m). **Time:** **4 h** total (1.5 h van). The walk itself is about 2 h. **Difficulty:** Low / Easy.
- **On the Las Torres list:** Yes. Stand-alone rate CLP 105,000 ([page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/los-cuernos/)). This confirms the guide's existing "Mirador Cuernos half day (~6 km, low)" alternative.
- **Overlap:** Salto Grande is also a stop on day 14. Mirador Cuernos itself is new.
- **Alternative Easy:** the guide's existing horse-ride alternative. Las Torres lists the Lake Nordenskjöld / Ascencio Valley ride as **half day 8 km, 3 h, Easy** or **full day 13 km, 6 h, Easy** ([Swoop](https://www.swoop-patagonia.com/chile/torres-del-paine/hotels/torres/excursions)). The guide's "~6 h" matches the full-day version. Riding experience needed is not published **[unverified]**.

### Recommended Moderate — French Valley, turning back at Campamento Italiano
- **What you do:** The same van, boat and trail as Full, along Lago Skottsberg to Campamento Italiano at the mouth of the French Valley, then back. The steep 2.5 km climb through forest and rock to the viewpoint is skipped. The guide's itinerary already notes Italiano as the "turn-back option".
- **Distance:** about **15 km** round trip. Paine Grande → Italiano is 7.5 km per a torresdelpaine.com trail description (cited in search). In the guide's GPX, the climb starts at about km 7.4. **Climbing:** about **350–400 m total** (GPX: about 255 m on the way out and about 115 m on the way back). Most of the day's climbing is on the part that is skipped. **Time:** about 5 h walking **[estimate]**, but **still a 12 h day** because of the boat. **Difficulty:** Moderate. It is longer than the 13 km target but has much less climbing. Mostly rolling lakeside trail.
- **On the Las Torres list:** only as part of the French Valley excursion. It needs **a second guide**, or the whole group turning at Italiano. **Ask.**
- **Extra cost:** none expected. The boat is part of the excursion.
- **Honest trade-off:** it saves the legs, not the hours. If the Moderate group would rather be back by mid-afternoon, use **Patagón** instead:
  - Las Torres half day: van 40 min to the Sarmiento gate, then **9.5 km on mostly flat pampa**, with Aonikenk rock paintings and guanacos. 4 h. Leaves the hotel at **07:45**.
  - Las Torres rates it Easy–Medium (tools list: Easy). Climbing is not published **[unverified; described as flat]**.
  - Sources: [Patagón page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/patagon/), [Las Torres blog](https://blog.lastorres.com/patagonia-tours), [tools list](https://tools.lastorres.com/excursions-videos).

## Other options considered

- **Windy Pass turnaround (day 13).** GPX: 4.4 km each way, 8.8 km and about 370 m round trip. It is the steepest part of the lower trail but not a destination. Chileno is only about 1 km further and mostly downhill-to-level. Chileno is the better turnaround. Not on the list.
- **Base Torres by horse + hike (day 13).** Las Torres: ride 9.2 km to Chileno and back, then hike 9.6 km to the viewpoint and back. 6–8 h, High, CLP 250,000 stand-alone ([page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/las-torres-viewpoint-horseback-riding-hiking/)). It keeps the hardest section (the final boulder slope), so it is not an easier level. It suits someone who wants the Towers view but not the lower valley. The tools list mentions "intermediate riding experience". **[Ask whether it is included in the programme.]**
- **Cuernos Sector Trail (day 13 or 15).** 23.2 km, 6–8 h, Medium–High ([page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/cuernos-sector-trail/)). Not easier. This confirms the guide's note.
- **Lakes Trail (any day).** 15 km, 7–8 h including 2.5 h in the van, Moderate. Climbs to a summit above Lago Toro ([page](https://lastorres.com/en/circuitos-por-el-dia/lakes-trail/)). A reasonable Moderate by rating, but long in the van and 15 km. Climbing is not published.
- **Encantado Valley horse ride.** 24 km, 6–8 h, High, for experienced riders. Not suitable.
- **Paine Grande → Mirador Lago Grey (day 15).** 11 km round trip, 3 h, moderate ([Stingy Nomads](https://stingynomads.com/best-day-hikes-torres-del-paine-chile/)). Starts with a 3 km uphill to Laguna Los Patos (search summary). Climbing is not confirmed. Not on the Las Torres list, and it would need its own guide off the French Valley boat. It also repeats the Grey view from day 14.
- **Mirador Cóndor (day 15).** 2.4–4 km round trip, 200–253 m, 1–1.5 h. Rated easy by Stingy Nomads but "steep" and moderate by others ([The Photo Hikes](https://thephotohikes.com/mirador-condor-hike/), [Stingy Nomads](https://stingynomads.com/best-day-hikes-torres-del-paine-chile/)). Sources differ on whether it starts at Paine Grande or at the Lago Pehoé side. Not on the Las Torres list. It could fill the wait at Paine Grande for a turnaround group if a guide goes with them **[unverified trailhead]**.
- **Catamaran round trip + short walk at Paine Grande (day 15).** Only possible return times are 09:20/11:20 or 17:00/18:40. Out on the first boat and back at 11:20 gives about 2 h at Paine Grande, but the van for the main group would not be at Pudeto then. It would need separate transport. Low value compared with the Cuernos half day, which uses the same pier area. Not recommended.
- **Mirador Británico (day 15).** Adds roughly another 4 km each way beyond Mirador Francés (sources vary). Not realistic within the boat times for this group. Not part of Full.
