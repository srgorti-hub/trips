# Hike levels research: El Chaltén and El Calafate (days 9–11)

Researched 28 Sep 2026. Purpose: give each hike day an **Easy** and a **Moderate** option alongside the planned **Full** day, for a 19-person group travelling late November 2026.

Level targets used:

- **Easy**: about 8 km or less, 250 m or less of climbing, no steep or exposed sections. Boats, vehicles and viewpoints count. Someone unfit or with bad knees can do it.
- **Moderate**: about half the Full effort (8–13 km, up to 500 m), or a turnaround point on the Full route.

Notes on sources:

- AllTrails pages block automated reading, so AllTrails figures below come from search-result summaries of those pages. Treat them as indicative.
- Trail figures vary between sources, mostly because some measure from the town centre and some from the trailhead. Ranges are given with the source for each end.
- "Unverified" means I could not confirm it from a source and it needs checking with the agency or guide.

## Day 9 — Fri 27 Nov, El Chaltén

**Full (as planned):** Laguna Torre out-and-back, 17.3 km, 533 m ascent, about 7 h, private guide from Walk Patagonia, meeting 08:30 at Desierto Suites. Booked through Say Hueque.

Useful fact for splitting the group: on the Laguna Torre trail, almost all of the climbing is in the first 3 km, up to Mirador del Torre. After that the trail is mostly flat along the Fitz Roy river valley, with a short rocky climb over the moraine at the end ([Patagonia Scout](https://patagoniascout.com/hikes/laguna-torre/)). So the Moderate group does most of the Full day's climbing but only about 40% of the distance.

### Recommended Moderate: Laguna Torre trail to Mirador del Torre and back

| | |
|---|---|
| What you do | Walk the first part of the Full route with the rest of the group, as far as Mirador del Torre (views of Cerro Torre, Cerro Solo and the Adela range), then return the same way. Cascada Margarita, at about 1.5 km, is on the way. |
| Distance | 7 km return from town ([elchalten.com](https://elchalten.com/v4/en/mirador-del-torre-trek-el-chalten.php), [Calafate Tours](https://calafate.tours/en/blog/guia-trekking-el-chalten-2026)). The viewpoint is 2.5–3 km from the start ([Patagonia Scout](https://patagoniascout.com/hikes/laguna-torre/): 2.5 km; other guides: 3 km). AllTrails' version from Calle Las Loicas: 6.8 km (4.2 mi). |
| Climbing | 150–290 m. Calafate Tours: 150–180 m. AllTrails (Las Loicas start): 289 m (948 ft). |
| Time | 2–2.5 h walking (AllTrails), 3–4 h including stops (elchalten.com). |
| Difficulty | Low (elchalten.com). A steady climb out of the valley at the start. It is the steepest part of the Full route, but short. |
| Start | Laguna Torre trailhead at the end of Calle Los Charitos, near La Torcida campsite. Two trailheads join within 5 minutes' walk. |
| Fits the booking? | Yes on the trail itself: it is the Full route with an early turnaround. But someone has to lead the group back (see "Splitting the group" below). |
| Extra cost | None if walked on its own. The park fee covers the whole day (see below). |

This is below the Moderate distance target (7 km rather than 8–13 km). The climbing is close to the Full day's first section, because that is where the Full day's climbing is. I still recommend it: it is the natural turnaround on the same trail, and it has the day's main view if the weather is clear. For a longer Moderate, walk on past the viewpoint along the flat valley section for 30–60 minutes and turn back. That adds distance without adding much climbing. How far it adds is unverified; the guide can set a turnaround time.

### Recommended Easy: Chorrillo del Salto waterfall

| | |
|---|---|
| What you do | A mostly flat walk through lenga and ñire forest to a waterfall north of town. It can be shortened by driving to the roadside parking on Ruta Provincial 23. |
| Distance | About 4 km return from the RP23 parking. 6.6 km return from the north edge of town ([jtreks](https://jtreks.co/el-chalten/articles/chorrillo-del-salto)). 7 km return (Calafate Tours). One source puts the parking–waterfall path at about 1 km each way ([mindtrip](https://mindtrip.ai/attraction/el-chalten-santa-cruz/chorrillo-del-salto/at-dEBkfny2)). |
| Climbing | Under 100 m (jtreks). "Flat" (Calafate Tours). |
| Time | About 50 min return from the parking. About 1 h 40 min from town (jtreks). |
| Difficulty | Low. Described by jtreks as the valley's only genuinely easy hike. |
| Start | The parking area on RP23, about 4 km north of town (about 10 minutes by car), or on foot from the north end of town past the campsite (signposted). |
| Fits the booking? | Needs a vehicle, or a walk from town. Not part of the Walk Patagonia booking. Ask Say Hueque for a taxi or van (unverified whether they can arrange it). |
| Extra cost | Park entry fee (see below). A car or taxi if not walking from town (price unverified). |

Chorrillo del Salto is reached through the Base Fitz Roy park entry point ([trekkingelchalten.com](https://trekkingelchalten.com/en/trail-access-fees-in-el-chalten/)).

**Other Easy choices for day 9:**
- **A town morning.** Rest, cafés, the National Park Visitor Centre at the south entrance to town. No cost, no logistics.
- **Lago del Desierto half-day trip** (boat on the lake, 37 km north of town). See "Other options considered". It is more of an outing than a walk, and it needs its own operator booking.

## Day 10 — Sat 28 Nov, El Chaltén to El Calafate

**Full (as planned):** Mirador de los Cóndores and Mirador de las Águilas, 7.4 km, 236 m, 2.5 h, guided by Walk Patagonia at 09:00. Then about 3 h private transfer to El Calafate (Hostería Glaciares de la Patagonia).

### Recommended Moderate: same as Full

The Full day is already within the Moderate range, so Moderate is the same as Full. Figures from other sources for the loop: 5.5 km from the trailhead with 226 m of climbing, plus about 2 km of walking to and from accommodation ([Kitti Around the World](https://kittiaroundtheworld.com/los-condores-and-las-aguilas-hike-in-el-chalten/)). Calafate Tours lists 4–6 km, 100 m, 2 h, low difficulty. The guide's 7.4 km / 236 m matches the loop plus the walk from town.

### Recommended Easy: Los Cóndores only, or a town morning

**Option A: Mirador de los Cóndores only (turn back before Las Águilas)**

| | |
|---|---|
| What you do | Walk the first part of the Full route with the group to the Los Cóndores viewpoint above town, then return the same way while the others continue to Las Águilas. |
| Distance | 2.5–2.6 km return from the trailhead (Kitti Around the World: 2.5 km; AllTrails, via search summary: 2.6 km). |
| Climbing | 130–215 m (AllTrails: 130 m; another source: 215 m). |
| Time | About 1 h return. About 40 minutes uphill to the viewpoint. |
| Difficulty | Low, but it is the steepest part of the loop (Kitti Around the World), and all of it is uphill on the way up. Coming down is the hard part for bad knees. Walking poles help. |
| Start | Los Cóndores trailhead at the National Park Visitor Centre, at the south entrance to town ([elchalten.tur.ar](https://elchalten.tur.ar/mirador-de-los-condores-y-aguilas/)). |
| Fits the booking? | Yes: same trail, same start time, early turnaround. The route back is short and in sight of town (see "Splitting the group"). |
| Extra cost | Park fee (Los Cóndores entry point). |

This is within the Easy distance and climbing targets but is not free of steep ground. For anyone who should avoid a steep descent, use Option B.

**Option B: Town morning (no hike)**

Pack, walk the town, visit the Visitor Centre, have coffee. This group joins the others at the transfer to El Calafate. No cost, no logistics. If they did not go on day 9, Chorrillo del Salto (above) also works as a flat morning walk, but only if a vehicle is available and the timing fits before the transfer.

## Day 11 — Sun 29 Nov, Perito Moreno Glacier

**Full (as planned):** private guide (Huellas del Sur), 08:00 pick-up, 1 h Nautical Safari boat from Puerto Bajo las Sombras, then the walkways (pasarelas). The guide lists 3.6 km and 174 m.

The boat is about 1 hour, from Puerto Bajo las Sombras (Ruta 11, km 70.9, about 7 km from the glacier). It stops about 400 m from the south face. Departures 10:00, 11:30 and 14:30. The operator (Hielo & Aventura) describes it as suitable for all ages ([El Calafate tourism site](https://www.elcalafate.tur.ar/en-glaciar-perito-moreno/safari-nautico-es.htm), [Hielo & Aventura](https://hieloyaventura.com/en/tour/safarinautico-en/)). The Safari Náutico does not include hotel transfer; the private guide's vehicle covers that.

### Walkway circuits

Figures differ between sources:

| Circuit | Length | Notes | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accesible (accessible) | 565 m (one source: 500 m) | Designed for wheelchairs and reduced mobility. Ramps and an elevator to the first balcony. About 30 min. | [Calafate Tours walkways guide](https://calafate.tours/en/blog/pasarelas-glaciar-perito-moreno), search summaries of viajeroscallejeros.com |
| Central | 400–600 m | The classic head-on view of the north and south faces. The busiest circuit. Calafate Tours says it has ramps and the elevator and calls it the easiest. | Calafate Tours; viajeroscallejeros.com |
| Bosque (forest) | 570–600 m | Among trees. "Few stairs" (Calafate Tours) / "medium" (other sources). | Calafate Tours; viajeroscallejeros.com |
| Inferior (lower) | about 1,100 m | Goes down closest to the ice. Many stairs; the most demanding. | Calafate Tours; viajeroscallejeros.com |
| Costa (coast) | about 1,117 m | Links the lower parking and harbour area to the north balcony. Long stretches, many stairs. | Calafate Tours; viajeroscallejeros.com |

Total network: 4–5.5 km depending on source ([peritomorenoglaciertour.com](https://peritomorenoglaciertour.com/footbridges/), [Tolkeyen Patagonia](https://tolkeyenpatagonia.com/en/the-perito-moreno-glacier-walkways/): 4.7 km).

A free internal shuttle runs every few minutes from the lower parking to the top of the walkways (about 5 minutes). This saves the steep uphill walk from the lower parking. One source gives its hours as 10:30–17:30 ([peritomoreno.com](https://www.peritomoreno.com/plan-your-visit/), plus travel blog search summaries). The park has a wheelchair that can be borrowed for the walkways; a companion is required ([Parques Nacionales, official](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/parquesnacionales/patagonia-austral/parque-nacional-los-glaciares/servicios)).

### Recommended Moderate: same as Full

Full is already easy by the group's targets (3.6 km, 174 m, on boardwalks with railings and rest areas). Moderate is the same as Full. The one change I would offer is to **skip the Inferior (lower) circuit**. It is the one with the most stairs, and it is the source of most of the climbing on the way back up.

### Recommended Easy: boat, then Central and Accessible circuits only

| | |
|---|---|
| What you do | The same Nautical Safari boat as everyone else. Then at the walkways, stay on the top level: the Accessible circuit (elevator and ramps) and the Central balcony, which have the full frontal view of the glacier. Skip the Inferior, Costa and Bosque circuits. Wait at the café or the balconies while others walk the lower levels. |
| Distance | About 1–1.2 km of boardwalk (Accessible 565 m + Central 400–600 m), plus the boat. |
| Climbing | Minimal. Elevator and ramps (Calafate Tours). Exact figure unverified. |
| Time | About 1 h on the walkways, plus the 1 h boat. |
| Difficulty | Easy. Suitable for reduced mobility. |
| Start | Top of the walkway network (upper parking, or the free shuttle from the lower parking). |
| Fits the booking? | Yes. Same vehicle, same guide, same boat. The group simply splits at the walkways and meets at an agreed time and place. No second guide is needed: the walkways are a fenced, marked, single site. |
| Extra cost | None beyond the planned park fee. |

Unverified: whether boarding the boat at Puerto Bajo las Sombras involves stairs or a ramp down to the dock. Ask Huellas del Sur if anyone in the group has limited mobility.

## Park entry fees (days 9–11)

- Los Glaciares National Park charges foreign visitors per day. One pass covers all trails that day. **Card or online only**; cash is not accepted at the El Chaltén entry points ([trekkingelchalten.com](https://trekkingelchalten.com/en/trail-access-fees-in-el-chalten/)).
- Sources disagree on the rate. ARS 45,000 (Calafate Tours rates page, "in effect since January 2025"). ARS 50,000 for 2026–27 (trekkingelchalten.com; [Calafate Tours walkways guide](https://calafate.tours/en/blog/pasarelas-glaciar-perito-moreno), "effective 1 June 2026"). Roughly US$30–35 per person per day. Confirm with Say Hueque whether park fees are already included in the guided days.
- El Chaltén has three entry points: Los Cóndores (Cóndores, Águilas), Base Fitz Roy (Torre, Fitz Roy, Chorrillo del Salto) and Río Eléctrico ([Patagonia Trekking](https://patagoniatrekking.com.ar/santa-cruz/cobro-acceso-senderos-el-chalten-parque-nacional-los-glaciares/)).
- Conflict: one report says the park superintendent kept Laguna Torre free when entered from the upper part of town ([Ahora Calafate](https://ahoracalafate.com.ar/contenido/34803/pn-los-glaciares-se-restablece-el-cobro-de-acceso-en-la-zona-nort), via Patagonia Trekking). trekkingelchalten.com says Laguna Torre is charged. Unverified which is current.

## Splitting the group on the same day

- **El Chaltén trails are marked and are commonly walked without a guide.** Trail guides list Laguna Torre, Mirador del Torre, Los Cóndores and Chorrillo del Salto among El Chaltén's self-guided trails ([Calafate Tours, "14 self-guided trails"](https://calafate.tours/en/blog/guia-trekking-el-chalten-2026)). The only registration rule I found is for Hito Limítrofe at Lago del Desierto (border control). I found no rule requiring a guide on these trails. So an Easy or Moderate group walking back to town alone on the same trail is allowed.
- **Is it safe?** The turnaround sections (Mirador del Torre, Los Cóndores) are the parts of each trail closest to town, on busy, well-worn paths. The main risks are wind, a slip on the descent, and someone setting off without the rest. Practical rules: walk back in pairs or more, never alone; one person carries a phone with the hotel address; agree a return time. Mobile coverage on the trails is unverified.
- **Leading the split groups.** A single private guide can only be with one group. For day 9 (the biggest split), options are:
  1. Book a second guide from Walk Patagonia for the Easy/Moderate group. Best option. Cost unverified.
  2. The guide walks everyone to Mirador del Torre. The Moderate group walks back on its own; the guide carries on with the Full group.
  3. The Easy group does Chorrillo del Salto or a town morning on their own, with no guide.
- **Guide-to-walker ratio.** With 19 people, the planned Full day may already need more than one guide. I could not find a published ratio for Santa Cruz province (unverified). Ask Walk Patagonia how many guides are booked.
- **Day 11** needs no extra guide. The walkways are one enclosed site with a café and shelter, and the group meets at a fixed time.
